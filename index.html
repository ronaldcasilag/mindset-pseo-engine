How we choose tools

Every recommendation is judged on the same three questions.

  • Does it do the job?We check what a tool actually does against what it claims, and who it suits.
  • What does it really cost?We note pricing, free-plan limits, and anything that locks features behind a higher tier.
  • What are the trade-offs?Every tool has weaknesses. We state them plainly so you can decide for yourself.
